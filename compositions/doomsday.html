SO WHICH WEEKDAY IS IT?
Finding 2026’s doomsday
1
The anchor day for the whole of the 2000s
TUESDAY
2
Last two digits, plus a quarter of them, rounded down
26 + 6 = 32
3
Whatever is left after taking out sevens
32 mod 7 = 4
4
Count that many days on from the anchor
TUE + 4 = SATURDAY
SO, IN 2026
4/4, 6/6, 10/10 and 12/12 are all Saturdays.
And 12 October is two days past 10/10 — so it is a Monday.
